## Overview

A full-stack, local supply-chain analytics platform that turns raw operational data into decisions - forecasting demand, classifying stockout risk (Low/Medium/High), and recommending reorder actions across 10,000+ records spanning sales, inventory, suppliers, and orders.

## Key Features

- **Automated model benchmarking**: A training pipeline benchmarks 4 tree-ensemble models (XGBoost, LightGBM, Random Forest, Gradient Boosting) with cross-validation and auto-selects the best performer per task.
- **Decision layer**: Converts predictions into actions - reorder timing and quantity with a 7-day safety buffer, supplier flagging, and demand-swing simulation.
- **Automated feature engineering**: Generates features across 4 data domains for real-time decision support.
- **Interactive dashboard**: A 14-page Streamlit app with drill-downs across sales, inventory, suppliers, and orders.

## Technologies Used

- **Python, scikit-learn, XGBoost, LightGBM, CatBoost** - modeling
- **SQLAlchemy** - data layer
- **Streamlit** - interactive analytics dashboard

## Outcome

The platform closes the loop from prediction to action, giving planners clear, data-backed reorder and supplier recommendations across thousands of SKUs.
